What clients say

Questions, answered.

How long does a website take?

Most business websites take 4–8 weeks from discovery to launch. Landing pages can be ready in 1–2 weeks; larger e-commerce builds take longer. You get a timeline before we start.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and content. After a free discovery call you get a fixed written quote — no surprises, no hidden fees.

Is the website audit really free?

Yes. It uses Google Lighthouse and our own scanner on your live site. There's no obligation — use the report yourself or ask us to fix the issues.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

Yes. We can set up an easy content editor and show you how to use it, or handle updates for you on a care plan.

Will a redesign hurt my Google rankings?

Not when it's done properly. We map every old URL, set redirects, and keep your best-performing content so rankings are protected.

Do you work with businesses outside my country?

Yes. We work remotely with clients internationally and can build in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese and German.

Do I own the website?

Completely. Once the project is paid, the design, code, content and domain are yours.
